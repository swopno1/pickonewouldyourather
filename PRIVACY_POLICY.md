# Privacy Policy for Pick One! – Would You Rather

**Last Updated: October 2026**

**ViveScript Solutions LLC** ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details our privacy practices regarding our mobile application **Pick One! – Would You Rather** (the "Application").

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collected by ViveScript Solutions LLC

**Pick One! – Would You Rather does not directly collect, record, transmit, sell, or store any personal data.** 

- **No User Accounts:** You do not need an account, email address, password, or profile to play.
- **No Sign-In:** There is no Google Sign-In, social authentication, or phone verification.
- **No Cloud Database:** All questions, vote tallies, and state are bundled directly within the app and operate locally on your device.
- **No Direct Identity Tracking:** We do not track personal identifying information, IP addresses, names, locations, or biometric details.

---

### 2. Device Storage & Local Preferences

The Application stores your momentary round progress and gameplay settings (such as sound mute/unmute and language preferences) solely within local in-memory/device storage. This information never leaves your mobile device and is cleared upon uninstalling or clearing the app cache.

---

### 3. Network Access & Offline Functionality

- The core gameplay is **100% offline-ready**. An active internet connection is never required to answer questions, browse categories, or view percentages.
- The standard Android Share functionality only triggers your device's native sharing sheet when you explicitly tap "Share" or a social action button (e.g. WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok) to send question text to your friends.

---

### 4. Advertising & Monetization (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

The Application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements:
- **Advertising Provider:** Google LLC (AdMob).
- **Data Processed by Google Play Services / AdMob:** Google AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP address for diagnostic coarse geo-location, and diagnostic/performance telemetry for fraud prevention and ad serving.
- **Privacy Practices:** All advertising transmission is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. For details on how Google processes advertising data, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **No Personal Profiling by App:** We do not associate AdMob identifiers with any personal user data or names.

---

### 5. Permissions

Pick One! requests only minimal non-sensitive permissions:
- `VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when you tap a choice card (only if sound/haptic is enabled).
- `INTERNET`: Required for Google Mobile Ads network communication and system social share intent dispatch.

No dangerous runtime permissions (such as Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, SMS, or Storage) are requested.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Because the Application features curated, family-friendly questions and does not collect personal identifiers, it complies with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and the Google Play Families Policy.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in this document and reflected in future updates of the application.
